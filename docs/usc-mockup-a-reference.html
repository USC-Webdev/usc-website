USC Donation Pool

Three ways to contribute. Only one of them involves money, and that one is optional.

3.1

Time

Playtime is not mandatory, but the org relies on its members' participation. The USC has a one-org policy.

3.2

Talent

Members contribute their skills, such as commerce, diplomacy and engineering, toward the group's goals.

3.3

Treasure

In game

After official launch, a standard tax will fund org assets such as capital ships. A Javelin, for example, does not buy itself.

Out of game

Patreon donations support general needs. Merch purchases send 20% to the general support fund. Non-monetary donations such as stickers and printed works are also accepted. Out-of-game donations fund meetups, social media and member rewards.

Patreon Swag Store

Membership never requires payment.

Donations are voluntary and confidential. Anyone asking for donations on the USC's behalf should be reported to the Consul of Peace.
